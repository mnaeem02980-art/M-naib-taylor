BESPOKE EXCELLENCE

The Art of Master M. Naib

With over a decade of hands-on atelier mastery in Kohat City, Master M. Naib has built a sterling reputation among executives, scholars, and wedding parties. We reject generic mass-stitching in favor of individual anatomical draping.

01

Anatomical Pattern Cutting

Every cloth pattern is drafted according to shoulder slope, chest curvature, and posture to prevent fabric pulling and wrinkles.

02

Thermo-Fused Anti-Bubble Bukram

We strictly import high-grade interlinings to ensure collars and cuffs stay razor-sharp wash after wash.

03

Reinforced Pocket & Button Anchor

Double-reinforced pocket corners and hand-stitched anchor knots that never tear under daily smartphone weight.
